Artist proposition

Seva Hifi is an Aotearoa-based three-piece creating Pacific psychedelic soul and dub through deep bass, percussion, machine rhythms and spacious electronic production. Led by Niuean vocalist, drummer and songwriter Baz Suamili, alongside Lebanese Kiwi producer Gmuva and Fijian percussionist Levani Vosasi, the group drives urgent songs of pressure, spirit and survival through dense rhythm, deep space and electronic sound.

The album

Pacific Psychedelic Soul is shaped by dub influence, soul, percussion and spacious electronic production, with 13 tracks for the digital album and 17 tracks for the upcoming vinyl album.

Rooted in Aotearoa New Zealand and shaped by Pacific sensibility, the record moves between urgency and reflection. City pressure sits beside oceanic space. Spiritual memory meets electronic sound. Intimate songs open into wide ocean space.

The album draws on Niuean heritage, Pacific polyrhythms, old-school soul, reggae, broken beat, jazz and studio experimentation. It is an intense listen, built for close attention, deep feeling and full physical presence.

The artist

Baz

Vocals · Keyboards · Drums · Guitar · Additional drum programming · Songwriting

Born and raised in South Auckland to Niuean parents from Tamakautoga, Baz Suamili began drumming and singing through church and his father’s family band. His music brings Niuean language, Pacific polyrhythms and spiritual roots into dialogue with synthesisers, samplers and drum machines.

Gmuva

Production · Drum programming · Electronics · Guitar · Blues harp · Songwriting

Gmuva, also known as Gareth Farry, is an Auckland-based Lebanese Kiwi producer and founder of Sugarlicks, a label centred on electronic soul with a Pacific-meets-global sensibility. He shapes the project’s spacious electronic production, deep bass and beat architecture, and wider sonic vision.

Levani

Percussion · Vibes

Fijian-born and based in Auckland, Levani Vosasi is one of Aotearoa’s most respected percussionists. He has performed with artists throughout New Zealand and alongside international acts including Baaba Maal and the Rolling Stones.

Live presentation

Seva Hifi is developing Pacific Psychedelic Soul into a live three-piece performance built around vocals, percussion, electronic production and deep rhythmic interplay. The show brings the intensity and spaciousness of the record into a physical setting, with Baz Suamili at the centre, Gmuva shaping the electronic architecture, and Levani Vosasi driving the live percussion.

The group is preparing for selected performances ahead of a wider international release and touring period in 2027–28. The live set is suited to festivals, club stages, arts programmes and curated events seeking a distinctive Pacific electronic sound with strong visual and rhythmic presence.
