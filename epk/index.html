Artist proposition

Seva Hifi is an Aotearoa-based three-piece creating Pacific psychedelic soul and dub through deep bass, percussion, machine rhythms and spacious electronic production. Led by Niuean vocalist, drummer and songwriter Baz Suamili, alongside producer Gmuva and Fijian percussionist Levani Vosasi, the group drives urgent songs of pressure, spirit and survival through dense rhythm, deep space and electronic sound.

The album

Pacific Psychedelic Soul Vol. 1 is a 13-track record shaped by dub influence, soul, percussion and spacious electronic production. The album carries songs of pressure, memory, blessing and release through machine rhythms, layered percussion and shifting intensity.

Rooted in Aotearoa New Zealand and shaped by Pacific sensibility, the record moves between urgency and reflection. City pressure sits beside oceanic space. Spiritual memory meets electronic sound. Intimate songs open into imagined future worlds.

The album draws on Niuean heritage, Pacific polyrhythms, old-school soul, reggae, broken beat, jazz and studio experimentation. It is an intense listen, built for close attention, deep feeling and full physical presence.

The artists

Baz

Vocals · Keyboards · Drums · Songwriting

Born and raised in South Auckland to Niuean parents from Tamakautoga, Baz Suamili began drumming and singing through church and his father’s family band. His music brings Niuean language, Pacific polyrhythms and spiritual roots into dialogue with synthesisers, samplers and drum machines.

Gmuva

Production · Electronics · Guitar · Songwriting

Gmuva, also known as Gareth Farry, is an Auckland-based producer and founder of Sugarlicks, a label centred on electronic soul with a Pacific-meets-global sensibility. He shapes the project’s spacious electronic production, deep bass and beat architecture, and wider sonic vision.

Levani

Percussion · Vibes

Fijian-born and based in Auckland, Levani Vosasi is one of Aotearoa’s most respected percussionists. He has performed with artists throughout New Zealand and alongside international acts including Baaba Maal and the Rolling Stones.

Live presentation

Seva Hifi is developing Pacific Psychedelic Soul Vol. 1 into a live three-piece performance built around vocals, percussion, electronic production and deep rhythmic interplay. The show brings the intensity and spaciousness of the record into a physical setting, with Baz Suamili at the centre, Gmuva shaping the electronic architecture, and Levani Vosasi driving the live percussion.

The group is preparing for selected performances ahead of a wider international release and touring period in 2027–28. The live set is suited to festivals, club stages, arts programmes and curated events seeking a distinctive Pacific electronic sound with strong visual and rhythmic presence.
